## Task: write the WEEKLY RECAP

For someone who was not watching this week and wants the through-line, not the log.

**Length: up to 160 words.** That is a budget, not a target — and it is emphatically **not**
permission to enumerate. A list of merges is not a report.

The question you are answering: **what is now possible that was not possible on Monday, and what is
still owed?**

- **Group by theme, never by commit or by repo.** Three changes that together made one flow work are
  one theme and one sentence. Repos are an implementation detail the reader does not think in.
- **Lead with shipped and closed work.** The evidence pack gives you epics whose status flipped this
  week, with their retrospectives — that is the highest-altitude product input available, written in
  product language by the people who did the work. Use it before you touch commit subjects.
- **Name the decision that shaped the week**, if there was one. Something chosen over an obvious
  alternative, something deliberately declined, something that turned out to be false and changed
  the plan. A week usually has exactly one of these and it is usually the most valuable sentence in
  the report.
- **State what is still owed** — unverified work, gaps a person has to close by hand, anything
  built but not yet live. The owed ledger is sacred here: a weekly that quietly omits a known gap is
  the one unforgivable error in this house.
- **A quiet week is a fact.** Report it in a sentence and stop.
