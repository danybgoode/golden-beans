⑤Git & Releases — legibility, not git educationnew surface

Not "Graphite for PMs." A translator that sits on top of it.

Graphite's real lesson is turning a confusing DAG into a clean, linear, status-at-a-glance picture — pointed at "is my stack mergeable." A PM doesn't need that question answered; they need a simpler one: what did my agent ship, in what order, and is any of it still waiting on something. Buzz's lesson layers on top: every entry is attributed to an accountable actor, agent or human, the same way the audit trail already tags every write. No rebase, no branch management exposed — that stays the agent's job.
